Your Postgraduate Journey

Five phases and fifteen steps, from admission to completion — exactly as set out in the department's Postgraduate Journey Roadmap. Five steps are decision gates: you must pass before moving on.

Phase 1

Coursework & Research Preparation

Admission and university registration, prescribed lectures (MSc), then formal departmental supervisor allocation.

Steps 1–3
Phase 2

Proposal Stage

Draft proposal to your supervisor(s), proposal-defence form (SPGS guidelines plus departmental additions), then defence at the monthly seminar with the Internal Examiner present or represented.

Steps 4–7
Gate at Step 7 · fail → back to Step 5
Phase 3

Research Execution & Progress

Progress report presented at the departmental monthly seminar, with the Internal Examiner present or represented.

Step 8
Gate at Step 8 · repeat / fall back as outcome requires
Phase 4

Thesis Verification & External Examination

Compliance & quality-assurance inspection by the Departmental Thesis Inspector, plagiarism check at the Library or ITMS (fees apply), then external defence.

Steps 9–11
Gates at Steps 9 & 10
Phase 5

Post-Defence Corrections & Completion

Comment-by-comment response to examiners' comments, final compliance inspection, submission of signed copies, completion.

Steps 12–15
Gate at Step 13

Step 2 is for MSc students only. The proposal defence and progress report both hold at the departmental monthly seminar. Inspector clearance is required twice — before the plagiarism check and before final submission. See the full PG Journey Roadmap for every requirement and outcome.

Please note — what this site is for

This site is developed strictly to assist students in navigating their postgraduate journey, and to facilitate departmental PG coordination, progress monitoring and documentation.

It does not replace official University processes. For any other matter — including anything requiring referral to the School of Postgraduate Studies (SPGS) — please contact the Head, Department of Physics or the PG Coordinator.

About the Postgraduate Journey

The department's own platform for tracking and managing postgraduate progress — built for students, the PG Coordinator and the Head of Department.

Who We Are

The Department of Physics, Faculty of Science, University of Abuja trains postgraduate physicists across MPhil, MSc and PhD programmes, with research strengths in five specialisation areas spanning solid-state, nuclear, solar and applied geophysics.

🏛

A Departmental Tool

The University already runs a central portal for postgraduate students across all departments. This platform is the department's own addition — focused only on how our students progress.

📈

Non-Result Progress

It manages everything before the transcript: admission status, lecture attendance checkpoints, supervisor allocation, proposal milestones, submissions, inspections and final compliance.

🤝

Built for Coordination

Designed so the PG Coordinator and Head of Department can oversee every student's journey, while students see exactly where they stand at any time.

What the Portal Does

  • Students register online, check their registration status, sign in to a personal dashboard and follow their 15-stage journey.
  • The PG Coordinator approves registrations, allocates supervisors, manages staff entries, posts announcements and reviews submissions.
  • The Head of Department has a read-only overview of the department's postgraduate cohort and progress.
  • Staff record supervisory notes and stage updates; submission inspectors review, approve or reject submitted work with comments.

The Journey

Every student moves through fifteen defined stages — the portal shows exactly which one you are on.

  • 1 – Admission  ·  2 – Lecture Attendance  ·  3 – Supervisor Allocation
  • 4 – Proposal Submission  ·  5 – Proposal Defence Form  ·  6 – Proposal Defence
  • 7 – Progression Clearance  ·  8 – Progress Report  ·  9 – Compliance Inspection
  • 10 – Plagiarism Check  ·  11 – External Defence  ·  12 – Examiners Comments
  • 13 – Final Compliance  ·  14 – Signed Copies  ·  15 – Completion
