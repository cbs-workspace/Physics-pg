1. Self-registration: the student fills in the form. Their account is created as Pending with a random password nobody knows, and they get a “registration received” email. They can’t sign in yet.

2. Approval: when the PG Coordinator approves them, one at a time or in bulk, the system generates a one-time password. It emails it to the student with their registration number. At first sign-in the student is forced to choose their own password.

3. If the email fails (mail quota or a bad address), the Coordinator sees a pop-up with the one-time password to pass on, so it is never lost. A Coordinator can also trigger “Reset password” for a student, which emails a fresh one-time password.

4. Students added by the Coordinator (Add Student or CSV import) skip the approval step. They get an account-created email with their one-time password straight away.

5. A student who never receives the email can use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. It sends a 6-digit code to the email on file, and the student then sets a new password.
