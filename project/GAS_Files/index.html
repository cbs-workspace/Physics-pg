Research Execution & Progress

Phase 4

Thesis Verification & External Examination

Approval for the final examination, thesis/dissertation submitted and checked (format, similarity, Inspector and PG Coordinator), then the External Examiner is assigned.

Steps 11–13
Gates at Steps 12 & 13
Phase 5

Post-Defence Corrections & Completion

External Examiner's comments, comment-by-comment response, final corrected version, final compliance check, signed copies and completion.

Gate at Step 15

About the Department

The Department of Physics, Faculty of Physical Sciences, University of Abuja trains postgraduate physicists across MPhil, MSc and PhD programmes, with research strengths spanning experimental and theoretical solid-state, nuclear and solar physics and applied geophysics.

Please note — what this site is for

This site is developed strictly to assist students in navigating their postgraduate journey, and to facilitate departmental PG coordination, progress monitoring and documentation.

It does not replace official University processes. For any other matter — including anything requiring referral to the School of Postgraduate Studies (SPGS) — please contact the Head, Department of Physics or the PG Coordinator.
